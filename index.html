The Machine

Built for
Extreme Rivers

Our custom-built jet boats are engineered for performance in the shallow, fast-moving rivers of the Altai. With no propeller to snag on rocks, they fly through inches of water at speeds that defy imagination.

  • Jet propulsion — no exposed propeller, safe in shallow rapids
  • Marine-grade hull — handles Class IV rapids with ease
  • 8-passenger capacity — perfect for groups and families
  • Full safety kit — life vests, helmets, first aid on every trip
